    What the checkpoint means

    The checkpoint is the end of the highest consecutively accepted range, beginning at the network’s approved first required index. A range is included only when its saved result indices are consecutive and its record integrity and FLINT/Arb verification metadata validate.

    Finite numerical computations do not prove or disprove the Riemann Hypothesis. A same-method recheck is not an independent mathematical proof.
